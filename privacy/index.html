Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Raise AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you visit www.raiseai.in or register for our webinars, and how we protect and use that data.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for our masterclasses or contact our support team, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: Full Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp / Phone Number.
  • Transaction Records: Payment confirmation timestamps, transaction reference IDs, and registered course name.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, and anonymous analytics to improve website user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Sending private webinar join links (Zoom), reminders, and calendar invitations.
  • Delivering the complete recorded masterclass and prompt resources following session completion.
  • Providing customer support regarding payments and registration inquiries.
  • Ensuring compliance with payment processing and fraud prevention standards.

4. Payment Security

All financial transactions are handled securely through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways and UPI protocols. Raise AI never collects, views, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, banking passwords, or UPI PINs.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or disclose your personal information to third-party marketing companies. We only share information with essential service providers (e.g., payment gateways and communication tools) strictly necessary to deliver webinar access to you.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and analytics tools (such as Meta Pixel and Google Analytics) to measure conversion performance and improve our educational services. You can adjust cookie preferences in your browser settings at any time.

7. Data Security & Retention

We implement standard encryption and administrative safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
