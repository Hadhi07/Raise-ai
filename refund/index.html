Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

At Raise AI, we are committed to providing world-class online educational training. We want every attendee to feel completely confident in their registration for our live masterclasses and digital resources.

2. Cancellation Policy

Because webinar admissions grant reserved seats in live interactive sessions and instant digital booking confirmations, registrations cannot be cancelled once processed. However, if you are unable to attend the live session, you will automatically receive the complete HD video recording + all prompt toolkits to keep forever.

3. Refund Eligibility & Satisfaction Guarantee

We offer a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. Refunds are considered under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate Payment: If you were charged more than once for the same registration due to technical or network issues, duplicate charges will be refunded in full.
  • Event Cancellation: If Raise AI cancels or indefinitely postpones a masterclass without offering an alternative date or recording, you will receive a full 100% refund.
  • Major Technical Failure on Our End: If a technical outage on our broadcasting infrastructure prevents the delivery of the workshop and recording, you will be eligible for a full refund.

4. Refund Request Process

To request a refund under eligible circumstances, please contact our support team within 48 hours of transaction:

  1. Send a message to WhatsApp Support (+91 73560 03301) or email support@raiseai.in.
  2. Provide your registered Full Name, Phone Number, Email, and Payment Transaction Screenshot / Reference ID.
  3. Our support team will review and verify your request within 24 to 48 hours.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, Card) within 5 to 7 business days, in accordance with standard banking and payment gateway settlement cycles.

6. Contact Support

For any queries concerning refunds or billing assistance, reach out directly to:

  • WhatsApp Support: +91 73560 03301
  • Email: support@raiseai.in
